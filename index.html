The Core Rule
Eat and salt before you need it
You're a cramper who wings it — that's the combo that breaks people on a 4,500ft climb in June heat at altitude. The summit will have you sweating hard before you feel it, and altitude suppresses hunger right when your body needs fuel most. Set a watch timer and execute the plan regardless of how you feel.
Key mindset shift: By the time you feel a cramp or bonk coming, you're already 20–30 minutes behind. Fuel and salt on a schedule, not on symptoms.
Salt / Electrolytes
Your #1 priority
The aid stations carry Tailwind (electrolytes in the drink) but don't rely on that alone given your cramping history. Carry your own salt chews as the foundation.
Hot / Hard effort
Every 30 min
If you're sweating hard on the climbs, go every 30. Don't wait.
Cooler / Easier pace
Every 45 min
Minimum interval. Still stick to the schedule.
Total to carry
8–12 chews
Enough for a 4–6 hour effort. Take one at the start line before you even feel warm.
Front-load it. Pop a salt chew at the start line even before you feel warm. The climb starts immediately.
Good products: SaltStick Chews, Precision Hydration chews, Nuun Sport tablets in your water bottle.
Energy Gummies & Gels
150–200 cal/hour minimum
The race provides Neversecond gels at every aid station — use those as bonus fuel, not your primary plan. Carry your own stash so you're never dependent on reaching the next station.

Real Food Snacks
What to pack in your vest
High elevation + sustained effort = your stomach gets picky. Stick to easy-to-digest, not overly sweet, doesn't melt. Test anything new before race day.
🌴 Medjool Dates
Fast carbs, easy on the stomach, portable, natural sugars
🥜 Mini PB&J Quarters
Real food that settles well, fat + carb combo for long efforts
🥔 Salted Potato Chips
Doubles as salt + carbs — great mid-race reset, easy to eat moving
🌾 Bobo's Oat Bar
Dense but not too rich, holds together well, real ingredients
🍌 Banana Pieces
Fast carbs + potassium, easy to digest if you tolerate them
❌ Avoid
High-fiber, dairy, anything new, heavy protein bars, greasy or spicy
